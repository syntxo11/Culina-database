Project Idea: Recipe Sharing Platform
This is a food app where people can share recipes from different countries. You can see what others cook and also post your own recipes.
There will be a map, and when you click on some region, you can see food from there. So it's also about culture and food together.

What the app should do:
1. User information
Every person needs to make an account. We save their name, email, where they live (city), and password.
Also, users get levels, like in a game, if they post more recipes. Higher level gives them new cosmetics.

2. Recipes
Users can add their recipes. A recipe has(name,ingredients,instruction how to cook it)

*if they want, they can write some tags like "vegan" or "low calories"
They can also choose if recipe is public or only for themself.

3. Ingredients
Each recipe has ingredients inside. We also want to show what type it is (like meat, vegetable, etc), so it’s more easy to search.

4. Tags
Users can put tags on their recipes. Tags help to filter, for example “no sugar” or “gluten free”.

5. Favorites and collections
People can click favorite on recipes they like. Also they can make collection, like “My Summer Recipes” or “Quick Lunches”.

6. Ratings
If you try recipe, you can give stars. Other people will see this rating.
If many people like recipe, it will have good score.

7. User levels
When user share more, they get points and their level grows. When they are high level, they can make their profile more cool (like colors or icons).

Why this app is cool:
It’s not just about cooking, but also about sharing with other people. You can learn new foods, and also show what you like to cook.
Also, it's good when you don’t know what to eat — this app can help.
